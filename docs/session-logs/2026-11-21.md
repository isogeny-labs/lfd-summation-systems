  docs/ACTIVE.md + draft PR #44, branch claude/index-calculus-root-finding-e95kd6.
- Built experiments/ic-rootfind-yield/: funnel baseline (yield_baseline.py, 5 seeds, prime
  n=11..23, m=3/m=4, LRF cross-checked vs enumeration, 0 mismatches), Frobenius orbit measurement
  (frob_orbits.py, real Koblitz curve a=0,b=1, orbit columns over E[ell]), Frobenius-span probe
  (frob_span.py), figure (make_graph.py -> yield_report.svg/png), report.pdf/report.tex.

### Decisions made (all evidence-backed)
- Objective is cost/relation = (systems+candidates)/relations, not yield/system (which ignores
  factor-base size and candidate cost).
- found_per_cand (roots-in-V) is the dominant, intrinsic yield loss; cost/relation ~ 2^(0.7 n).

### Results (measured)
1. Optimal operating point is a square system (delta=k+dimV2-n in {0,1}), m=3; m=4 strictly worse
   at every matched (n,k). At n=131, m=3 yield forces k~=44 = delta 0, so the paper is already at
   the optimum.
2. found_per_cand is the same narrow band for every LRF-feasible basis at fixed (delta,n)
   (monomial, w^2-powers, shifted); random bases have dim V2 = n (infeasible). No factor-base
   lever changes the exponent.
3. Frobenius NO-GO at prime n, with mechanism: monomial V's points lie in distinct orbits so the
   realizable column reduction decays to 1 (1.40 at n=11 -> 1.09 at n=23) while the ideal grows to
   n; a Frobenius-structured sliding-window V has real orbit sharing but dim V2 saturates at n and
   LRF is infeasible for k >= ~sqrt(2n). The dim-V2 (< n) vs Frobenius-structure requirements are
   in irreducible tension.

### Open threads (where an improvement could still come from)

### Results (measured, decisive negative)
- ANF degree of the good-indicator is ~delta (maximal, random-like) at delta=6,9,12; density ~2^-delta.
  The good candidate is an unstructured singleton per system -> no output-sensitive enumeration.
- found_per_cand loss is not recoverable by candidate selection; raising delta buys ~1 relation per
  solvable system at 2^delta candidate cost, so small delta (0-1) is optimal (the baseline point).
- Full verdict: every lever (factor base, summand count, Frobenius orbits, candidate structure) leaves
  the ~2^(n/3) cost/relation exponent unchanged. An improvement needs a genuinely different relation.
