## ECC2K-130 window question: exact invariants of the Weil factor A

### What was done
- Owner said "Discover it" on the one open route from the transfer survey. Computed the exact Frobenius
  char polynomial of the 130-dim Weil-restriction factor A of ECC2K-130, directly (not by citation).
  P_W(x) = x^262 - s_131 x^131 + 2^131; P_A = P_W/(x^2+x+2). Script:
  experiments/ecc2k130-weil-factor/compute_A.py; polynomial in P_A.json. Writeup:
  docs/ecc2k130-weil-factor-A.md. Tracked in PR #51.

### Results (exact, cross-checked)
- s_131 = -22283658519494248867; #E(F_2^131) = ...940516, cofactor exactly 4.
- #A(F_2) = 680564733841876926932320129493409985129 = P_A(1), PRIME (130 bits) = the ECDLP prime r.
- P_A: degree 260, constant 2^130, squarefree, middle coeff odd => A ORDINARY (p-rank 130).
- CM field K = Q(zeta_131, sqrt(-7)), degree 260 (sqrt(-7) not in Q(zeta_131) since 7 does not divide 131).
- A SIMPLE: direct proof that alpha*zeta has trivial Gal(K/Q)-stabiliser (alpha/beta=(-3-sqrt-7)/4 has
  trace -3/2, not an algebraic integer, not a root of unity), so [Q(alpha zeta):Q]=260=deg P_A =>
  P_A irreducible. mod 3,5,13 each give two degree-130 factors, consistent with Gal = C_130 x C_2.

### Decisions / findings
- The easy hope (A splits into a smaller target) is disproved: A is simple.
- A has a rational order-131 automorphism; a genus-130 degree-131 cyclic cover of P^1 exists (RH: 4 branch
  points), so the automorphism is NOT an obstruction. The open discriminating question is a CM-type
  match: does A's CM type equal that of a cyclic-cover Jacobian factor? A's field contains sqrt(-7) beyond
  Q(zeta_131); producing that extra piece is what the crypto survey's genus>=394 bound is about.
