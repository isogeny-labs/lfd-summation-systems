## ECC2K-130: cyclic-cover construction rigorously excluded (sqrt(-7) obstruction)

### What was done
- Owner: "work on solving it". Attacked the one decidable family (cyclic/abelian covers of P^1).
  Proved A is not an isogeny factor of any such cover in the usable genus window. Script:
  experiments/ecc2k130-weil-factor/cyclic_cover_exclusion.py.

### Result (rigorous)
- Frobenius eigenvalues of an abelian cover's Jacobian (exponent N, char 2) lie in Q(zeta_N) (Jacobi
  sums, Weil). A's eigenvalues generate K=Q(zeta_131,sqrt-7); embedding K into Q(zeta_N) forces
  917=lcm(7,131) | N.
- Verified exactly: alpha^130 = (73000192456347156057 - 4061839345336951189 sqrt-7)/2 has nonzero
  sqrt(-7) part, so alpha^130 not in Q(zeta_131) (Q(sqrt-7) cap Q(zeta_131)=Q since 7 nmid 131);
  |alpha^130|^2=2^130. So a pure Z/131 cover (eigenvalues in Q(zeta_131)) cannot carry A -- the missing
  piece is the sqrt(-7) from E's CM.
- Genus: 917 | N => tame Z/917 cover of P^1 has genus >= 458 (r=3), far above the window (~130-300).
  Contrast: Z/131 cover r=4 has genus 130 in-window but no sqrt(-7). => cyclic covers excluded; clean
  mechanism for and sharpening of the crypto-notes >=394 bound.

### Decisions / scope
- This closes all abelian/cyclic-cover constructions (the practically usable ones). It does NOT close
  the fully general case (A a Jacobian of a curve with no useful automorphisms); that has no known
  decision procedure at dim 130 and stays open.

### Open threads
- Fully general Jacobian question: open (no algorithm). All construction families with abelian symmetry
  now excluded. Any realization needs cover order divisible by 917 (genus >=458) or a non-symmetric curve.

## ECC2K-130 cover-of-E: literature grounding + cohomological structure

### What was done
- Searched literature (Scholten; Freeman ePrint 2009/103; cover-attack surveys). Confirmed the corrected
  status and clarified the cohomology of the Prym route.

### Findings (literature + rigorous structure)
- Scholten: Res(E) IS a genus-2 Jacobian for n=2; genus-3 cover attacks for n=3. Freeman: Res(E) is
  "generally not itself a Jacobian" for higher degree; must find a curve whose Jacobian is isogenous.
  Converse: a curve mapping nonconstantly to E has Jac isogenous to Res(E) = our reduction.
- GHS cover of P^1 for ECC2K-130 is genus ~2^130 or the zero map (prior survey); does NOT give the
  genus-131 object. A non-GHS degree-131 cover of E is the open question, right at the genus floor >=130.
- Cohomological structure: Prym eigenvalues come from RAMIFIED order-131 twists of E. An unramified
  nontrivial rank-1 local system on genus-1 E has H^1=0 (chi = rank*(2-2g)=0), contributes nothing; a
  twist ramified at 2 points has chi_c=-2, giving 2 eigenvalues per character, 260 total = 2*dim Prym.
  So A's pairs {alpha zeta^t, beta zeta^t} must be H^1(E,rho^t) eigenvalues of a ramified character --
  concrete existence condition, no cheap obstruction. (Corrected my earlier wrong "unramified signature"
  intuition.)

### Open threads
- Existence of the ramified order-131 character / genus-131 cover of E with Prym ~ A: OPEN. Needs
  cohomology/polarization theory or a specialized search; F_2^131 too big to brute-force. Also:
  indecomposable principal polarization on W (W only isogenous, not isomorphic, to E x A).
