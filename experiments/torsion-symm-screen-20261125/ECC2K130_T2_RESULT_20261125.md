# ECC2K-130 fixed-base torsion compatibility — 2026-11-25

**Result:** the configured Hamming-weight-two factor-base recipe is disjoint
from its image under translation by the rational order-two point. A direct
torsion-equivariant reuse of that base is therefore incompatible with the
frozen factor-base construction. This is a structural screen, not a solver
degree or ECDLP cost measurement.

## Exact recipe calculation

The [frozen protocol](PROTOCOL_ECC2K130_T2_COMPAT_20261125.md) reads
`isogeny-labs/crypto`'s fixed parameter JSON (SHA-256
`f41972681d260ef4eeb2fea7441f31859f4a121fdd5f2ecc4ff3dee54082aeae`)
and follows its `indexcalc_fixed.py` rule: Hamming weight is measured in the
type-II **normal basis**, despite the public curve points being written in a
polynomial basis. The inspected crypto commit was
`5097d0d45c46c72041ddacca012fe694252ded6d`; its `field.py` and
`indexcalc_fixed.py` SHA-256 values were respectively
`008d063064e53d3a971296a6ee659a02b0c2ea5834535bdbee153487f9d13d7c`
and `5459718ea15d5f0db66b03a693ce9a75a833650812918f05cfe8073efd0e9614`.

For `K_0: y²+xy=x³+1`, the rational two-torsion point `(0,1)` sends a
nonzero abscissa `x` to `1/x`. The complete recipe universe contains
131 weight-one and 8,515 weight-two nonzero ONB coordinates. Exact
reciprocal enumeration gave:

| Input ONB weight | Candidates | Reciprocal weight ≤2 | Minimum reciprocal weight |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 131 | **0** | 66 |
| 2 | 8,515 | **0** | 33 |
| Total | **8,646** | **0** | 33 |

Every candidate multiplied by its computed reciprocal to the field unit.
The ONB representation was checked against the declared polynomial and
18 independently computed polynomial-basis inverses, including boundary
indices. The first counterexample is ONB coordinate `0x1`; its reciprocal
has ONB weight 66. Because the *entire* recipe universe and its reciprocal
image are disjoint, any nonempty subgroup point set selected from it also
lacks a `+T₂` partner within the same recipe. The configured factor base
cannot be made pointwise `T₂`-closed without changing its membership rule.

The calculation also verifies `ord_131(2)=130`. Hence the only proper
Frobenius-invariant `F₂`-subspace of `F_(2^131)` containing 1 is `F₂`
itself. The native `u=1/(x+1)` construction requires such a subspace;
its directly transplanted proper-base form has only two field elements at
degree 131. This is a separate size obstruction, not a claim about all
nonlinear or tuple-level torsion-equivariant bases.

## Existing implementation and cost evidence

The crypto repository already implements a `koblitz-symmetrised` base and
`symmetrised` oracle with the `u` frame. Its archived
[end-to-end comparison](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/blob/5097d0d45c46c72041ddacca012fe694252ded6d/research/koblitz_symmetrised_e2e_20260927/RESULTS.md)
measured complete pipeline costs on smaller Koblitz fields. At `n=17,23,31`,
the symmetrised/plain `x` cost ratios were 0.84, 0.77/0.76, and 4.01 for
the completed `m=2` pairs, while all algebraic arms remained above their
matched rho references. The `u` and `x` factor bases differed in point
and column counts; for example, the `n=17` `m=2` hit rates were 0.317 for
`u` and 0.604 for `x`. Those archived measurements therefore do not establish increased
independent relation yield at controlled total base size or a lower exact
LFD on this paper's frozen systems. This new recipe screen must not be
presented as a new implementation of the already existing oracle.

The screen does not evaluate the exact selected point list's relation yield,
Boolean solver degree, root lifting, linear algebra or matched cold-rho cost.
Its zero-overlap proof is sufficient for the narrower compatibility verdict
above. A distinct equal-size factor base or tuple-level construction would
need its own frozen target stream and complete cost accounting.
