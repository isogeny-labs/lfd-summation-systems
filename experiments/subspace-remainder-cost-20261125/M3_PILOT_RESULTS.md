# Three-summand matched cost pilot

The [frozen protocol](M3_PILOT_PROTOCOL.md) and [manifest](m3_manifest.json) define three ordinary binary-curve cells. Each has two random prime-subgroup targets and one planted three-point target. Native LRF, direct three-point enumeration, and conditional-image LRF used identical factor bases and target points, in fresh native ARM workers. Every completed arm returned the same canonical signed triples; each triple was checked against the curve group law and the planted witness was found. All nine arm-by-cell repetitions completed.

The wall and CPU figures are complete worker totals, including solver construction, image-table construction, three queries, relation serialization, and group-law verification. The image/native-LRF wall ratios are approximately 0.32, 0.34, and 0.29. Raw per-target relation sets, statistics, wall/CPU receipts, peak RSS and source hashes are in [the compressed results](m3_pilot_results.json.gz). Decompress with `gzip -dc` before JSON analysis. Sparse modular coefficient-rank calculation was measured separately: approximately 0.049, 0.091, and 0.585 seconds in the first repetition of the three cells. The augmented-system rank matched coefficient rank. Its cost is common to all arms when they produce identical rows.

**Limits.** This is a three-target pilot, not a complete relation collection or scalar-recovery experiment. Every measured matrix has fewer rows than columns, so its factor-base logarithms are underdetermined. The common input generator was run and verified before timing but its wall/CPU cost was not recorded in the pilot results; this is a protocol-accounting gap to correct in the larger campaign. The original first invocation failed at its final RSS telemetry line; its receipts are preserved separately in [the amendment](M3_PILOT_AMENDMENT.md), excluded from the table. The image filter still enumerates the LRF linear-system kernel and does not reduce the polynomial's formal degree. The result supports a native relation-query speedup on these exact cells and motivates a full same-target relation-collection cost test, including failed queries, linear algebra, setup and a matched reference method. No larger-field conclusion follows.
